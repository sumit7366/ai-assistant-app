About AI Personal Assistant

What is This?

AI Personal Assistant is a smart application that helps you with face recognition and personalized interactions. It can recognize faces and speak personalized descriptions.

Features

  • Face recognition using camera
  • Text-to-speech for descriptions
  • Secure image upload and storage
  • Local data storage (no cloud required)
  • Responsive design for all devices

Technology Used

Flask Python JavaScript HTML/CSS

Privacy First

We believe in privacy. All your data is stored locally on your device. No data is sent to external servers unless you explicitly choose to.
